UN Investigative Mandates

helenarbc.com
Every country-specific investigation the Human Rights Council has mandated, 2006–2026 — the texts, the votes, the reports.
What is counted
Counted
Resolutions and decisions of the Human Rights Council that establish a country-specific investigative mechanism, renew it, widen it, or give it new tasks.
Not counted
Thematic mechanisms, country special procedures, and bodies mandated by the General Assembly, the Security Council or the Secretary-General. Each one is listed, with the reason, on the Methodology tab.
You get
For each text: the recorded vote state by state, its sponsors, and the mandate wording. For each mechanism: its reports, searchable paragraph by paragraph. Every figure links to the UN document behind it.
